# Code of Conduct

This applies to every space Lambdaforge runs — repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any channel that carries the org's name.

## The standard

We are here to build good tools together. That works when people can disagree about the work
without it becoming about the person. So:

- **Engage with the work, not the worker.** Critique code, designs, and decisions directly and
  specifically. Don't make it personal.
- **Assume competence and good faith.** Ask what someone meant before deciding they were wrong or
  careless.
- **Be precise, not cruel.** "This leaks a connection on the error path" is useful. Sneering is not.
- **Welcome people who are new to this.** Everyone wrote their first pull request once.
- **Respect a no.** When someone declines or asks you to stop, that's the end of it.

## Not acceptable

Harassment in any form; demeaning, discriminatory, or sexualized comments; targeting someone over
who they are rather than what they did; deliberate intimidation, stalking, or doxxing; sustained
disruption of discussion; or publishing others' private information without consent.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to a maintainer — message [@191-iota](https://github.com/191-iota) on GitHub.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will look into it and may warn, remove content,
or block a contributor from the org's spaces, in proportion to what happened. Maintainers hold
themselves to the same standard; a report about a maintainer goes to another maintainer.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short on purpose.
